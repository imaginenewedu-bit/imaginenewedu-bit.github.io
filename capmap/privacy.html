Capmap GPS Camera privacy policy Effective date: 4 October 2026 Developer: AKG.Edu Contact: imaginenewedu@gmail.com Capmap GPS Camera ("Capmap") is a camera app that stamps photos with their location and time and keeps them organised in projects. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: your photos, videos, locations, projects and reports stay on your phone. Capmap has no accounts and no servers of its own. The app is free and shows small banner ads, and the ad service collects some information, described below. Information that stays on your device Capmap stores the following only on your phone. The developer never receives it. Photos and videos you take in the app, including the location, time and compass bearing stamped on them. Photos are also saved to your phone's gallery under Pictures/Capmap. The location of each capture, its GPS accuracy, altitude and satellite count, and the place name looked up for it. A digital fingerprint of each photo, used to show later that the photo has not been altered. Project names, descriptions and settings. Reports, CSV and KML files you create. These are saved where you choose. If Android's device backup is switched on, your phone may include the app's project database in your own Google backup. That backup belongs to your Google account and is not accessible to the developer. Permissions Permission Why Capmap asks Camera To take photos and videos. Location (while the app is open) To stamp each photo with where it was taken. Capmap never uses your location in the background. Microphone Only to record sound with videos, and only if you use video mode. Internet To show banner ads, load map tiles, and look up place names. You can refuse any permission. The app keeps working with fewer features; for example, without location, photos are saved without coordinates. Information that leaves your device Capmap sends information off your phone only in these cases: Advertising. Capmap shows banner ads through Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your device's IP address (which can indicate your general location), your Android advertising ID, how you interact with ads, and app performance information. Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where the law requires it, Capmap asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice from the app's Settings. Maps. When you open a map, the app downloads map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Those requests reveal your IP address and the area of the map you are viewing. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. Place names. To show a place name on a photo, the app sends the photo's coordinates to Android's built-in geocoding service, which is provided by Google. The coordinates are used to return the place name and are not stored by Capmap anywhere else. When you choose to share. If you share a photo, video, report or file, it goes to the app or person you pick. What happens next is governed by that app's own policy. The developer does not sell your information and does not receive your photos, locations or files. All network connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Advertising ID You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings under Google > Ads (the exact path varies by phone). Keeping and deleting your information Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual photos or whole projects in the app. Uninstalling Capmap deletes the app's database. Photos and videos saved to your phone's gallery stay there until you delete them from the gallery. Because the developer holds none of your data, there is nothing to request deletion of from the developer; information collected by Google for advertising is governed by Google's policies. Children Capmap is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
